# 0625/21 — Cambridge IGCSE Physics, Paper 2 Multiple Choice (Extended), Oct/Nov 2021

## What this document is

`0625_w21_qp_21.pdf` — the Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 question paper for
Paper 2 Multiple Choice (Extended), October/November 2021, variant 21. Sixteen
pages: page 1 is the instructions cover, pages 2–16 carry the forty questions
(the paper states "There are **forty** questions on this paper"), and page 16
ends with the copyright notice. The published UCLES mark scheme is appended as
pages 17–19 and is marked `"role": "answer-key"` in `exam.json`.

All 40 questions were extracted. Every question is a plain four-option MCQ
(A–D). There are no matching questions, extended-matching stems or true/false
grids.

## Where the answers came from

**All 40 answers are `extracted`, every one from the appended mark scheme.**

Pages 18–19 are a plain three-column `Question | Answer | Marks` table: the
answer is a single printed letter beside the question number, questions 1–28 on
page 18 and 29–40 on page 19. Every `answer.evidence` names the mark-scheme page
and row it was read from. The question pages themselves carry no marks — the
paper is a clean, unwritten-on copy — so the mark scheme is the only answer
source, and it covers all forty.

Zero answers are `reasoned` and zero are `none`, so nothing is waiting for tutor
approval and every question should export straight away.

## Figures

34 figures cropped, each one opened and checked for clipping and for bleed from
the neighbouring question; several were re-cropped after the first look (the
q03, q04, q20, q22, q29, q37 tables lost their bottom border; the q15
thermometer lost its right-hand tip; the q26, q27 and q31 circuit rows were
clipped top and bottom).

Two conventions worth knowing when reviewing:

- **Where the document printed no option list, the options are `["A","B","C","D"]`
  and the table or drawing is the figure.** That covers every "Which row is
  correct?" table (q03, q04, q05, q10, q16, q18, q20, q22, q25, q29, q37, q39)
  and every question whose choices are four labelled pictures (q02 graphs, q11
  containers, q26 and q27 circuits, q30 logic-gate pairs, q31 circuits, q35
  graphs). The tutor reads the row or picture off the crop, exactly as a
  candidate does. Their rows were *not* flattened into option text.
- **A question with both a diagram and an option table has two figures**, cropped
  separately and listed in printed order: q05 (bridge + T1/T2 table), q16 (pan +
  materials table), q18 (wave + amplitude/wavelength table), q20 (ray diagram +
  table), q25 (sphere + charges table), q29 (I–V graph + table). Q36 likewise
  ships its two views ("view from side", "view from above") as two figures.

Every one of the 40 questions has its own `box`; none had to be left unbounded.

## Things worth a second look

- **The mark scheme's own header says "Paper 1 Multiple Choice (Extended)"**
  while the question paper says "Paper 2". Both documents are stamped 0625/21,
  October/November 2021, and the mark scheme's 40 rows match the paper's 40
  questions one for one, so this is a UCLES typo on their cover sheet, not a
  mismatched pair. Nothing was changed on account of it.
- **Fraction options were written with a solidus.** Q07's options C and D and all
  four of q09's are printed as stacked fractions; they are transcribed as
  `impulse = change in momentum / time`, `t = Fd / P` and so on. The words are
  the document's; only the fraction bar became a slash.
- **Subscripts and superscripts.** Printed subscripts on symbols (T₁, T₂, R₁, R₂)
  are written inline as `T1`, `T2`, `R1`, `R2` in the question text. Numeric
  superscripts in q13 are kept as Unicode (`80 cm³`, `2.0 × 10⁵ Pa`).
- **Q40's stem is just "Which statement is not correct?"** with the emphasis on
  *not* printed in bold. The bold is not carried into the plain text — read the
  question as testing the incorrect statement.

## Question boxes

Every one of the 40 questions has its own `box`. None is omitted, so no question
falls back to showing the tutor a whole page. Each box covers that question's
stem, its own diagram and option table, and its last option — and nothing of a
neighbour. Seven boxes (Q10, Q11, Q12, Q14, Q16, Q17, Q19) were widened after a
visual check showed the right-hand end of a full-width stem line clipped.

## Transcription notes

- Printed question numbers and A/B/C/D option labels are stripped; Codox adds
  its own.
- Q34's stem prints a numbered list of four tests — each item is on its own
  line, with blank lines separating the three parts of the stem, so the item
  boundaries survive.
- Superscripts and subscripts are carried as Unicode where the paper prints
  them (`mm²`, `mm³`, `3 × 10⁸`, `R₁`, `R₂`).
- Fractions the paper typesets stacked are written inline (`p = h / ρg`,
  `sin x / sin z = sin c`, `1/16`). The stacked printed form is still visible to
  the tutor inside the question's `box`.
- `year` is `"2021"` on every question, from the paper's own header
  (`0625/22/O/N/21`, "October/November 2021"). No topic headings are printed, so
  `topic`/`subtopic` are left blank and `topics[]` empty — the tutor matches
  against their own list after import.

# 0625_w20_qp_23 — Cambridge IGCSE Physics, Paper 2 (Extended)

**October/November 2020, variant 23.** 16 exam pages: page 1 cover, questions on
pages 2–14, page 15 printed BLANK PAGE, page 16 the copyright notice. The
published UCLES mark scheme is appended as pages 17–19 with
`role: "answer-key"`.

40 questions, numbered 1–40, matching the count the cover states. Every one is
a plain four-option A/B/C/D multiple-choice question — no matching questions,
no true/false grids, no extended-matching stems, nothing that needed splitting
or a `not_mcq` flag.

## Where the answers came from

All 40 from the **appended mark scheme**, which prints one answer letter per
row in a Question / Answer / Marks table (Q1–28 on bundle page 18, Q29–40 on
page 19). Every answer is `source: "extracted"` and its `evidence` names the
key page and row it was read from. Nothing is `reasoned`, nothing is blank, so
the paper exports from Codox with no tutor approvals needed.

The exam pages themselves carry no marks — this is a clean published paper, so
the mark scheme is the sole answer source, and it is complete.

**Independently cross-checked:** the mark scheme was read twice, once from the
rendered key page image and once from the PDF's own text layer. Both readings
agree on all 40 answers.

## Judgement calls

- **11 questions print no option list at all** — the choices are rows of a
  table ("Which row is correct?") or lettered items in a drawing: Q5, 12, 14,
  19, 21, 27, 28, 29, 33, 35, 38. These carry `["A","B","C","D"]` with the
  table or drawing cropped as the figure, per QUALITY.md. No table row was
  flattened into option text.
- **4 questions show two pictures** and got two separate crops, listed in
  printed order: Q19, 29, 33, 35. A diagram and its option table are never
  combined into one crop.
- **Q12's crop deliberately includes the key beside the table** (`✓ = needed`,
  `✗ = not needed`) — without it the ticks and crosses are unreadable.
- **Q14's options are lettered heights inside the barometer drawing** (A, B, C,
  D marked against the mercury column), so the drawing is the figure and the
  options are the bare letters.
- **Q23 is deliberately one crop, not two.** The car and the bus are drawn as a
  single plan view of one road scene — their relative positions are the content
  of the question — so splitting them would destroy what the picture says.
- **Q40's four nuclear equations stay as text options.** The paper prints a
  real lettered A–D list, so per QUALITY.md they are transcribed rather than
  cropped, using Unicode super/subscripts
  (`²⁰⁹₈₂Pb → ²⁰⁹₈₃Bi + ⁰₋₁e`). The printed form uses two-dimensional stacked
  notation and an italic *l* in thallium that plain option text cannot carry;
  the question's `box` shows the page exactly as printed. This is the one place
  in the paper where the option text is a linearisation rather than a literal
  copy.
- **Q35 reads `4800 V a.c..` with two full stops** — that is what the paper
  prints, not a typo introduced here.
- Statement lists in Q13 (1–4), Q15 (P/Q/R), Q20 (1–3) and Q32 (1–3) keep their
  line breaks, so the item boundaries survive.
- Superscripts, subscripts and Greek letters are carried as Unicode
  (`kg / m³`, `m / s²`, `v₁`, `²³⁴₉₀Th`, `γ-rays`, `β-decay`, `°C`), matching
  the page.
- 26 figures in total; 18 questions need no picture.
- The paper states **no topic taxonomy**, so `topics[]` is empty and every
  `topic`/`subtopic` is blank. Match against your own topic list in Codox after
  import. `year` is `2020` throughout.

## Crops

Every one of the 26 crops was opened and checked for both failure directions.
Two were re-cut before being accepted: `fig-04` (Q8) was cutting the `90 cm`
dimension label off the left edge, and `fig-07` (Q14) was clipping the mercury
trough and the `A` height off the bottom. No crop shows any part of a
neighbouring question.

## A note on the text layer

The PDF's text layer silently drops some symbols — Q18's `4.2 J / (g °C)` came
through as `4.2 J / (g C)`, and Q38's `²³⁴₉₀Th` was missing entirely. Both were
transcribed from the rendered page instead. Worth knowing if anyone re-derives
this paper from its text layer alone.

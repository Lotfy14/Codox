## Options that are `["A","B","C","D"]`

17 questions print no option *text* — the choices are rows of a table
("Which row is correct?") or lettered parts of a drawing. For those the letter
is the option, and the table or drawing ships as the figure so the tutor reads
the row off the picture exactly as a candidate does:

- **Option tables:** 2, 12, 18, 21, 23, 26, 27, 34, 35, 38
- **Lettered items in a drawing:** 3 (four speed–time graphs), 14 (heights A–D
  on the barometer), 24 (labelled sections of the wave), 29 (four field
  diagrams), 33 (four logic-gate circuits)

Question 12's crop includes the `✓ = needed` / `✗ = not needed` key printed
beside the table — without it the crop is unreadable.

Questions that *do* print a real option list keep it as text even where the
entries are terse (13's `10 / 100 / 1000 / 100 000`, 17's `1 only / 2 only …`,
28's `1 and 2 / 1 and 3 …`).

## Figures

30 crops, every one opened and checked for both failure directions (clipped
label, and any bleed from a neighbouring question). Questions with a diagram
**and** an option table get two separate crops, listed in printed order:

- q002 (graph + table), q023 (plan views + table), q033 (truth table + gates),
  q034 (generator + table), q035 (transformer + table)

Question 19's figure is the *data* table of height vs temperature — its options
are ordinary text; the table is the data the question is about.

Question 23's two plan views ("before" and "after" the shaft rotates) are one
crop deliberately: they are a single side-by-side illustration of one device,
the same shape as question 16's before/after strip.

## Transcription notes

- Statement lists inside a stem keep their line breaks — questions 15 (P/Q/R)
  and 17 (1/2/3) are three separate lines with a blank line before the prompt,
  not a run-on paragraph.
- Superscripts and subscripts are carried as Unicode where the paper prints
  them: `25 cm²`, `0.13 g/cm³`, `6.7 × 10⁻⁴ m`, `⁶⁸₃₀Zn` (q38 stem), and the
  four nuclear equations in question 40 (`²⁰⁹₈₂Pb → ²⁰⁹₈₃Bi + ⁰₋₁e`, …).
  Question 40 prints a genuine lettered option list, so it stays text rather
  than becoming a cropped picture; the printed stacked form is visible in the
  question's own `box`.
- Question 35's stem ends `into 4800 V a.c..` — two full stops, as printed.
  Not a typo introduced here.
- Cambridge's thin spaces inside quantities (`20 cm`, `1.5 mm`) are rendered as
  ordinary spaces.
